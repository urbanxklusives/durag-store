Bandanas

A bandana is the most versatile piece in the drawer: a square of fabric that works as a head wrap, a neck layer, a style accent or a quick hair cover. It does not train waves the way a durag does, and it does not protect overnight the way a bonnet does — its strength is versatility, and that is exactly why it belongs in the line.

Ways to wear it

Folded as a headband, tied as a full head wrap, worn at the neck, or used as a quick cover on a day when the full routine is not happening — the bandana adapts to the day. The fold decides the look: narrow fold for a clean band, full triangle for coverage.

Fabric and care

Most bandanas are cotton, which means they wash easily and dry fast — the opposite care profile from satin. Wash them with similar colours, and they hold up to daily wear. The trade-off to know: cotton pulls moisture, so a bandana is a style and coverage piece, not an overnight protection piece.

Styling with the rest of the line

A bandana sits naturally alongside durags in an outfit — same colour logic, same culture, different job. Match the colour story to the rest of what you are wearing and keep the fabric honest: cotton for the look, satin for the sleep.
