Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. This policy explains what durag.store collects, why, and the choices you have. durag.store is run from Brampton, Ontario, Canada. It will be updated when checkout goes live.

What we collect

  • Launch-list email address — only if you join the drop list, so we can send launch and restock notices. The signup form is handled by Netlify Forms; your address is stored with your form submission until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact-form details — your name, email address and message, only when you write to us through the contact page, so we can answer you. Messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them.
  • Order details — once the store opens: name, shipping address and order contents, so orders can be fulfilled. Payment is processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data — this site uses Google Analytics 4, which collects anonymous, aggregate information about how the site is used (pages visited, approximate location, device type) so we can improve it. Analytics data is not linked to your name or email address.

How we use it

To send the emails you signed up for, fulfil orders, answer questions, and operate and improve the store. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers that run the site itself (hosting and form handling by Netlify, analytics by Google).

Cookies and advertising

Google AdSense code is installed on this site. Ads will only serve once Google approves the site; until then no advertising cookies are set by AdSense. If ads do serve in future, Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and you can opt out of personalised advertising through Google’s Ads Settings, or by visiting youradchoices.ca (Canada) or aboutads.info (US). Google Analytics also uses cookies to count visits; you can opt out with Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. Essential cookies needed for the site to function may still be used.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsored or paid placements, if any ever run, will be clearly labelled. House ads for our own network sites (UrbanXklusives, OEM Center Caps, career e-books) are labelled as such and are not paid placements.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the email list at any time using the link in any email we send, or by emailing support@durag.store. To ask what personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, reach us through the contact page or at support@durag.store.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. The previous draft version of this policy is no longer in use; this page is the only privacy policy for durag.store.
